About us

Where the Adriatic
meets your plate

Tucked on the waterfront of Grebaštica — a quiet fishing village near Šibenik on the Dalmatian coast — HOBA bistro&bar is the kind of place that earns its place in travel memories. Local fishermen deliver the catch each morning; the rest is fire, salt, and craft.

Our wood-burning stone oven runs all day, producing perfectly charred pizzas, slow-baked fish and the octopus-under-peka that regulars drive hours to eat. We pair every dish with handpicked Dalmatian and Mediterranean wines — including the island whites that rarely make it beyond this coastline.

Summer evenings on the terrace, with the sea twenty steps away, live music drifting across the water and a cold glass of Pošip in hand — that is what HOBA is about.
